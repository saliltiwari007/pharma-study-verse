# Pharma Study Verse — Content Integrity & Information Disclaimer

## Purpose
Pharma Study Verse aims to provide useful, accurate and current educational and career information and makes reasonable efforts to validate important information against authoritative sources.

## Scope
This disclaimer applies across the platform, including notes, explanations, quizzes, assessments, games, career guidance, examination-preparation material, syllabus information, eligibility information, recruitment information, regulatory information and AI-assisted content.

## No guarantee
Pharma Study Verse does not guarantee that any information is complete, error-free, current at every moment, or suitable for a particular student's circumstances. No content should be interpreted as a guarantee of examination marks, rank, selection, employment, salary, admission, registration, licence, certification or career outcome.

## Source validation
Where practical, high-impact or time-sensitive claims should be checked against the most relevant authoritative source, such as the applicable statutory/regulatory authority, university/board, examination body, recruitment authority or government source. Source attribution and verification date should be retained for governed content where available.

## Changes over time
Syllabi, regulations, examination patterns, eligibility requirements, recruitment rules, fees, dates and other official requirements can change. A previously published item may therefore require re-verification. Superseded material should be versioned or marked accordingly rather than silently presented as current.

## AI-generated material
AI may assist with drafting, transformation, question generation, game generation, tagging, explanations and review. AI output is not automatically authoritative. Governed academic or consequential content must remain subject to the configured approval and publication workflow. Uncertain or conflicting claims should be flagged for human review.

## Student responsibility
Students should verify important decisions using the latest official notification or authority source applicable to their programme, jurisdiction, examination or recruitment. The platform is an educational guidance service and not a substitute for an official notification, professional advice or an institution's formal decision.

## Corrections
If an error or outdated statement is identified, the platform should provide a correction/review mechanism and update the governed source record. Accuracy is an ongoing process, not a claim of perfection.

## Suggested short site notice
"We make every reasonable effort to provide accurate, useful and source-validated information. Rules, syllabi, examinations, eligibility and career requirements may change. This content is for educational and guidance purposes and does not guarantee marks, rank, admission, selection, employment or career outcomes. For important decisions, please verify the latest information with the relevant official authority."
