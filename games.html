Our game design rule

Game first, question second

The student should have to do something—catch, move, match, sort, build, avoid, rescue or decide. MCQs can support a game, but they will not be the game itself.

Clear finish line

Every round will have an explicit objective, target count, lives or difficulty, scoring rule, end state and result feedback.
