Student Guidance

Practical advice for pharmacy students.

Focus on understanding mechanisms rather than rote memorization. Connect concepts across subjects.

Create a topic-wise plan, revise regularly, and practice with sample questions. Use checklists.

Understand procedures, practice explanations aloud, and review common viva topics.

Maintain subject-wise, unit-wise notes. Use highlighting and summaries for quick revision.

Set weekly goals, balance subjects, and track progress with a simple planner.

Stay informed about pharmacy career paths — industry, clinical, research, and regulatory roles.
