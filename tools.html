Tools Hub

Calculators, dictionary, career utilities and practical pharmacy tools—with transparent working, not just a final number.

Our rule: every calculation should be reproducible. The tool shows the formula, values used, intermediate steps, final answer and a quick sanity check. Clinical and professional outputs are educational aids, not a substitute for official standards, SOPs or professional judgement.
